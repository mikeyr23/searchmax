04 / GOOD QUESTIONS

Let’s make
this clear.

Is AI SEO different from regular SEO?

There’s significant overlap. Google’s AI search features build on established SEO practices. We start with that foundation, then consider platform-specific access and visibility checks. Read Google’s guidance.

What does the free review include?

A focused review of one issue or agreed area, evidence of what we checked, and a recommended first step. You can keep the one-page review. A 10-minute walkthrough is available, and implementation is a separate paid service.

How much does the pilot cost?

Pricing depends on the agreed work. You’ll receive a written scope, any setup fee, monthly pilot pricing, and regular renewal pricing before committing. Monthly payments are available during the 90-day pilot.

How will we measure progress?

We record a baseline and the work completed, then review search visibility alongside qualified calls, inquiries, and bookings where tracking is available. AI citations and recommendations are recorded separately. Sampled AI results can change and don’t prove a sale occurred.

Do you have client case studies?

We’re recruiting our first pilot clients, so we don’t have client case studies to publish yet. The sample review above demonstrates our approach. Any future client results will be shared with permission.

Do I need a monthly service?

Only when there is ongoing work worth doing. If the review points to a one-time fix, we’ll explain that. Continuing after the pilot is your choice.
